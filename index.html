Abstract

We propose a memory-efficient optimizer for training 3D Gaussian Splatting that preserves the underlying scene representation and reconstruction quality. We target the memory overhead of Adam when optimizing Spherical Harmonics, which account for a substantial fraction of training memory. By dropping the first moment, pooling the second moment across a Gaussian, and exploiting the low-rank structure of gradients, we achieve a 3.3× reduction in static memory, and a 1.7× reduction in peak memory on standard benchmarks that grows to 2.5× at scale. Our approach also lowers the memory bandwidth for the optimizer, resulting in 2.5× faster training at scale, all while matching the quality of Adam. This allows fitting up to 2.5× more primitives on the same GPU, unlocking large scale scene training with fewer resources. Our optimizer is a drop-in replacement and cleanly integrates into various training systems. In CPU-offloaded training, it can reduce the PCIe traffic by 3.7× and the CPU RAM requirement by 2.6×. In multi-GPU training, it reduces the memory per GPU by up to 1.7×.
